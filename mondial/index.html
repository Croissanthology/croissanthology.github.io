Chapitre zéro

Le football in 90 seconds

Everything you actually need. No jargon without a translation, promise.

Two teams of eleven. One ball. Ninety minutes. Whoever puts the ball in the other team's net more often wins — and you're not allowed to use your hands unless you're the goalkeeper, the one player dressed differently. That's genuinely the whole game. Everything else is detail, and the details are where it gets beautiful.

A football team is a little society: a goalkeeper guards the net, defenders protect the territory in front of it, midfielders run the economy — winning the ball, moving it, deciding the tempo — and forwards live for one thing only: goals. Tap around the pitch below to meet each tribe.

Interactive — the offside rule, finally

Offside is the rule everyone pretends to understand. Here it is, honestly: when your teammate passes you the ball, at that exact moment you must not be closer to the goal than the last defender (goalkeeper aside).
